Privacy Policy — OP Data

Last updated: September 2026

Data Collection

OP Data does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. All scanned barcode data and generated images are stored locally on your device and are never sent to any server.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use any analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party services. No account or login is required.

Device Permissions

The app requests access to:

  • Camera: to scan Data Matrix barcodes. Images are processed locally and never transmitted.
  • Photo Library (add only): to save generated Data Matrix images to your photo library. No existing photos are accessed or read.

These permissions are used solely for the stated purposes. No data is transmitted externally.

Data Storage

All app data (scan history, generated barcodes) is stored locally on your device. No cloud storage or synchronization is used. Uninstalling the app removes all associated data.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
